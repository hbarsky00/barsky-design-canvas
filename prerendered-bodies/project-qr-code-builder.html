What it does

AI Style Generation

Describe your brand, get a QR code that matches. Colors, shapes, and patterns generated to spec.

Built-in Analytics

Track scans by location, device, and time. Know which codes are working.

Brand-Safe Output

Every generated code is tested for scannability before export. Beauty doesn't break function.

One-Click Export

PNG, SVG, and print-ready PDF. No design tool required.

Why it exists

QR codes are everywhere and almost all of them are ugly. The brief was: what if the code itself was part of the brand? The design challenge is keeping AI-generated styles scannable — beauty can't break function.

What I haven't solved

This is a concept. The hard part is the scannability constraint — generative styles that look great but fail to scan are useless. That tension isn't solved yet.
