WHAT IT DOES

Reminders without the friction.

✓

Type the way you'd say it

"Call mom Tuesday 6pm" or "Gym Mon Wed Fri 7am" — it understands plain English. No forms, no dropdowns.

✓

Repeats that actually work

Weekly, every other week, weekdays, custom — just type it in a sentence and the app handles the rest.

✓

Get nudged your way

Pick how you want to be reminded: in the app, browser pop-up, email, or text message. On time, or 5 minutes early.

✓

Add it to your phone

Install it on your home screen like a real app — works on iPhone and Android. Reminders still fire when the tab is closed.

✓

Try before you sign up

Type a reminder first, make an account after. Your reminder doesn't disappear when you sign up.

✓

Keeps you safe

If you accidentally type a password or credit card number, it warns you before saving. Nothing private gets logged.
